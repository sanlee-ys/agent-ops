- **ask-user:** `_check_reset` hard-codes `origin`. A local-only repo on `main` has no `origin`. There, `git reset --hard HEAD~1` drops at least one commit, `ls-remote origin` fails, and the guard blocks with "cannot verify". Nothing can be published from such a repo. The same applies to any repo whose remote has another name. The fix is to treat a missing remote as "nothing published", or to resolve the branch's configured upstream remote. That is a behavior decision on the fail-closed exception, so please decide.
- **auto-fix:** `_push_target` does not resolve `HEAD` as the remote-side name. `git push --force origin HEAD` while on `main` gives `branch == "HEAD"`, which is not `main`, so the guard passes it silently. The result is the destructive shape the guard exists to catch. Resolve a `HEAD` destination to `_current_branch(repo)`. Also, `_FORCE_FLAGS` does not match combined short flags such as `-fu`, so `git push -fu origin main` is not seen as a rewrite. Match short-flag clusters that contain `f`.
